# BioKey

![BioKey]({{ '/assets/feature-graphic.png' | relative_url }})

An app lock for Android. The apps you choose open only after your face, fingerprint or screen
lock, and stay unlocked while you're using them.

**[Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nbdesigns.biokey)**

- Lock the apps you choose behind face unlock, fingerprint, or your PIN, pattern or password.
- Unlocked apps stay unlocked while you use them, and lock again when you leave: at once, after a
  delay you choose, or when the screen turns off.
- Group apps into profiles (Finance, Social, Work, or your own), each with its own re-lock delay.
- Optionally hide a locked app's notifications until you unlock it.
- Works under Android's Advanced Protection.
- No internet access. BioKey collects no data: what it keeps stays on your phone.

Requires Android 12 or later.
